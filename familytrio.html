About the trio

Oksana, Khrystyna, and Marta Skira perform as a mother-daughters ensemble across Toronto and the GTA.

The trio brings piano, flute, and violin to weddings, cultural events, private celebrations, holidays, and recitals. Their work grows from years of family music-making and professional training, with Khrystyna performing in wind ensembles and Marta serving as a string orchestra concertmaster.
