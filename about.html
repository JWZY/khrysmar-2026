Principal Instructor

Oksana Skira

Concert pianist, recording artist, and educator based in Toronto.

Oksana received her music education at the Music Collegiate in Drohobych, Ukraine, and holds an Honours Piano major from the Lviv State Musical Academy M.V. Lysenko, where she earned her Master's degree in piano performance.

She holds the Certificate of Competency and Qualifications as a concert pianist, chamber music ensemble soloist, concertmaster, accompanist, and instructor. In addition to teaching at Khrysmar, she runs Da Capo Academy of Music.

Every student arrives somewhere different. The work is to meet them there.

Philosophy

Patience. Curiosity. Craft.

Lessons at Khrysmar follow the student. We prepare for the milestones — RCM, auditions, competitions — but the heart of the work is consistent: careful technique, honest musicianship, and the steady joy of getting better at something you love.

Some students pass through one exam after another. Others come for the simple pleasure of playing a piece well. Both are equally welcome here.
